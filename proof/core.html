Step 2 · The desk core

Every call to a world goes through one door, WorldIO, which writes it to a journal before it leaves and its answer after it comes back. The books (State) are folded only from what the world said. After a crash, the desk replays its journal (no calls to the server, and every decision must come out the same, or it stops), recovers the one call that may have been in flight from the world's own snapshot and events, and goes on live. The policy here is still the kit's starter, so its week must equal the handbook's.
