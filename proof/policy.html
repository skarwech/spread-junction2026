Idea 1: price above list, win fewer cars

At level 8 the competitor charges 26–54% above list, so quoting list wins almost everything, far more than the drivers can carry. Raising the multiplier trades volume for margin and on-time share. The best multiplier differs by world.

Two hold-out weeks miss the 90% gate (89.0% and 86.7%). Fixed thresholds do not suit every world: the next idea is to adapt within the week, raising the price when the week's on-time share or driver backlog says the desk is taking too much.

What the runs taught about robustness

Some weeks did not reproduce. The cause was on our side of the wire: on an unstable network, the kit's client resends a request whose connection broke after the server had received it, so the server applied it twice (two gigs for one car, a clock moved twice, events lost). The desk now uses a client that never sends a POST twice and lets the core recover from the world's own state, and it refills any gap in the event sequence. With that, 18 kills at random moments still give the identical week (step 2 page).
